Products & Specifications

Available references include 0.22mm, 0.30mm, 0.40mm, 0.50mm, and 0.55mm thicknesses. Quality & Coating

Project inspection requirements can include checks for coating adhesion, color transfer, and surface uniformity. Because results also depend on the press, transfer paper, ink, settings, and application, discuss a sample plan and confirm the applicable inspection criteria in the written quotation.

Edge condition can vary with the shape, thickness, tooling, holes, corners, and finishing method. Tell us the front- and back-side edge requirements and tolerances for your application; finishing options and inspection criteria can be reviewed and confirmed in the drawing and written quotation.

While settings vary by press model, a general starting point: 180-200°C (356-392°F) for 60-90 seconds with medium pressure. We always recommend testing on sample blanks first to dial in the perfect settings for your specific equipment.
