Privacy & Cookie Notice

This notice explains how the business operating this website under the AlumCraft trade name handles inquiry information, technical data, analytics choices, and privacy requests.

Who we are

AlumCraft is the trade name used for this China-based aluminum blank supply website. It is not presented here as a separate incorporated legal entity. For privacy questions or requests, contact znegshifan@yushiglobal.cn.

This notice describes the website’s current technical operation. It is not a substitute for legal advice and may be updated when business or regulatory details change.

Information we handle

Inquiry information

When you submit the quote form, we receive your name, optional company, email, destination country, product interest, quantity, project details, website language, and the page used to submit the inquiry.

Campaign attribution, only after consent

If you accept analytics, we may associate the inquiry with session-level campaign information such as UTM parameters, an advertising click identifier, the landing page, and a privacy-filtered referrer. These values are treated as untrusted metadata and are not used to make automated decisions.

Technical information

Our hosting and security providers may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser type, page path, time, referrer, device identifiers, and operational logs. The Coze hosting platform also injects operational and page-view measurement code into deployed pages.

Why we use information

  • To answer your inquiry, prepare a quotation, and continue requested business discussions.
  • To deliver inquiry emails, prevent duplicate submissions, limit abuse, protect the website, and diagnose delivery problems.
  • With your consent, to understand which pages and campaigns generate useful inquiries and to improve promotion.
  • To meet applicable legal, accounting, security, or dispute-handling requirements.

Inquiry processing is necessary to respond to your request and for our legitimate business and security interests. Optional analytics and advertising storage rely on your consent where required.

Service providers and transfers

  • Coze hosts and deploys the website and may process technical and operational analytics data.
  • QQ Mail transports inquiry emails to our sales inbox.
  • Google supplies web fonts and, only when configured and allowed by your choice, Google Analytics or advertising measurement. See Google's business data privacy information.
  • WhatsApp / Meta processes data under its own terms when you choose the WhatsApp link.

These services may process data in countries outside your own. We do not sell inquiry information or send form names, email addresses, company names, free-text messages, or quantities to Google Analytics or Google Ads.

Cookies and browser storage

Our consent tool applies globally and allows you to accept or reject optional measurement. Rejecting optional storage does not stop you browsing the site or sending an inquiry.

How long information is kept

Inquiry emails and related business correspondence are retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, support the business relationship, meet legal or accounting duties, and handle disputes. Rate-limit and duplicate-delivery records are held temporarily in server memory. Hosting logs and analytics data follow the applicable provider and account retention settings.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection, and you may withdraw optional analytics or advertising consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not use inquiry information for solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Contact and updates

Send privacy requests to znegshifan@yushiglobal.cn. To help us locate an inquiry, use the same email address that you used in the form. We may need reasonable information to verify a request.
